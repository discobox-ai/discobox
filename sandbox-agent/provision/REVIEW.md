# Provision Review

- **What measures the box's own traffic also sees the pool calling it.** The
  pool's check-in, its status wait and the reads a status change wakes cross
  the box's interfaces, a few KiB at a time. A count of what the box received
  (a step's progress, `received`) moves only past a floor above that traffic
  (`receivedFloor`), and reports what it held at that move: counted raw, a hung
  download shows as receiving, and each publish the pool's traffic caused
  wakes the pool to make more of it.
