# judge review notes

- Anything a job carries beside its evidence is bounded so the whole job stays within `MaxInput` next to a body shown at its worst (`MaxBodyBytes`, about 48 KiB escaped). `policy.Validate`'s own bounds allow some 256 KiB, so a policy is held to `MaxPolicyBytes` in `Validate`, where it enters (`PolicyFits`), and again where a grant is minted, on the policy as bound to its discobox: a value can be longer than its variable. A new place that mints a grant with a policy checks it there too. One that is not would be approved and then judge nothing: larger than `MaxInput` it fails every job under the grant, first round included, and a little smaller it passes the first round and fails the one that shows the body the judge asked for.
- A field added to a recorded eval case is checked by `TestTheRecordedCasesLoad` in `internal/cmd/discobox-judge-eval`, which `go tool task test` runs. A check that only a model-calling run performs is a check nobody runs.
- The judge evals build rule and proposal jobs through the same root-module functions as the server (`HoldToRule`, `WrittenPolicy`, `RuleProblem`, `UnderPolicy`), never a copy: copies of the rule check drifted within one change.
- A change to any of Jev's questions, criteria or thresholds (`jev/judge.go`) changes `jev.QuestionsVersion` in the same change. Nothing tests it, as `TestThePromptVersionNamesTheseWords` ties `System` to `PromptVersion`, so a reworded question slips through, and verdicts and eval runs then name one version for two questions.
- A fact the control plane derives from evidence the discobox wrote — a command's arguments — says only what the control plane established from it ("a discobox the command names by its ID"), never what the evidence will do ("would act on"). The discobox chooses the evidence, so a fact that claims more lets it plant a decoy carrying Discobox's authority. `System` says a discobox no fact describes has no creator to rely on; that lowers the odds a reference the facts do not follow gets through, and is a model's reading, not a guarantee.
